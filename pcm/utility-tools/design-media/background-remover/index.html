How to do am

  1. Upload picture, then choose Remove background to try Fine AI first. Switch to Original AI if you wan compare. For plain, checker or striped background, you fit use Colour detection too.
  2. Check di edge of di subject and holes inside for di Result side. Change preview background to white or dark colour so you fit see leftover edge pixels.
  3. Use Keep to bring back subject parts wey disappear, and Remove to clean background wey remain.
  4. Leave Smart selection on for area wey get clear edge. E go select only joined pixels and no cross another colour.
  5. For hair, thin lines and small details, off Smart selection, make brush smaller and fix di edge by hand. Use Clean marks, Undo and Redo anytime.
  6. When di transparent edge don clean, download di transparent PNG.

Smart selection dey pick only pixels wey join where you click and match your colour tolerance. Another colour go block am. You fit use am with Keep and Remove; click separate areas one by one. Red line show Remove, green line show Keep. You fit hide di lines, and dem no dey enter download. Keep area get priority. Use Clean marks to clear and draw again. Off Smart selection for one-pixel work.
